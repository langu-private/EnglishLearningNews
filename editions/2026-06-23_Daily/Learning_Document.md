## 2. Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)

Here are 8 key words and phrases extracted from the podcast to help build your vocabulary.

### 1. **Legacy** 
*   **Phonetic:** /ˈleɡəsi/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** n. 历史遗产，遗留问题，后世影响
*   **Example Sentence:** The retired president left a lasting legacy of peace and economic growth. (这位退休的总统留下了和平与经济增长的深远影响。)

### 2. **Domestic**
*   **Phonetic:** /dəˈmestɪk/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** adj. 国内的，家庭的
*   **Example Sentence:** The government is trying to boost domestic consumption to support local businesses. (政府正试图扩大国内消费以支持本土企业。)

### 3. **Resign**
*   **Phonetic:** /rɪˈzaɪn/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** v. 辞职，辞去（职务）
*   **Example Sentence:** Due to health reasons, the CEO decided to resign from his position. (由于健康原因，首席执行官决定辞去职务。)

### 4. **Mandate**
*   **Phonetic:** /ˈmændeɪt/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** n. 命令，指令，强制要求
*   **Example Sentence:** The school issued a mandate requiring all students to wear uniforms. (学校发布了一项强制规定，要求所有学生穿校服。)

### 5. **Market value**
*   **Phonetic:** /ˈmɑːrkɪt ˈvæljuː/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** n. 市值，市场价值
*   **Example Sentence:** Apple became the first company to reach a market value of three trillion dollars. (苹果成为第一家市值达到三万亿美元的公司。)

### 6. **Crude oil**
*   **Phonetic:** /kruːd ɔɪl/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** n. 原油
*   **Example Sentence:** The price of crude oil has a direct impact on gasoline prices at the pump. (原油价格对加油站的汽油价格有直接影响。)

### 7. **Compete**
*   **Phonetic:** /kəmˈpiːt/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** v. 竞争，对抗
*   **Example Sentence:** Small local shops find it hard to compete with giant online retailers. (本地小商店发现很难与大型在线零售商竞争。)

### 8. **Wipe out**
*   **Phonetic:** /waɪp aʊt/
*   **Chinese Meaning:** v. phr. 彻底摧毁，消灭，使（资产等）化为乌有
*   **Example Sentence:** The sudden stock market crash wiped out all of his savings. (股市的突然暴跌使他的所有积蓄化为乌有。)

## 3. Useful Expressions (实用表达)

### 1. **A giant of a man**
*   **Explanation:** 字面意思是“巨人般的人”，在新闻和日常英语中，常用来形容**在某一领域极具影响力、举足轻重、受人尊敬的伟人或巨头**。
*   **Example:** Steve Jobs was a giant of a man in the technology industry. (史蒂夫·乔布斯是科技界举足轻重的人物。)

### 2. **Stand up for...**
*   **Explanation:** 意为**“支持，维护，为……挺身而出”**，通常指在面对压力或反对意见时，勇敢地捍卫某人或某种权益。
*   **Example:** You must learn to stand up for your own rights. (你必须学会捍卫自己的权利。)

### 3. **Rely on...**
*   **Explanation:** 意为**“依赖，依靠”**，是描述科技、经济或人际关系时非常高频的词组。
*   **Example:** Many modern businesses rely heavily on cloud computing. (许多现代企业严重依赖云计算。)

### 4. **A blow to...**
*   **Explanation:** 意为**“对……的打击”**。常用于商业和政治新闻中，形容某项政策或事件给某方带来了严重挫折。
*   **Example:** The new tariff was a major blow to international trade. (新关税对国际贸易是一个重大打击。)
