🎯 Grammar Focus
1. The use of "be able to" for ability in different tenses.
In English, we use "be able to" to talk about what a person or machine can do. In this podcast, we say: "You will probably not be able to get one yet." This is the future tense of "be able to". It is very useful when we want to talk about future possibilities or limits.
2. Conditional sentences with "if" to show cause and effect.
We use "if" to connect an action with a result. For example: "If the price of oil goes down, it is good for common persons." This structure helps ESL learners explain complex ideas about trade, money, and daily life using very simple connections.

(美联储设定货币支付的利率，我们称之为利率。)
