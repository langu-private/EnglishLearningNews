**We will start with the strong shaking of the earth in the Philippines. A great moving of the earth, with a measure of 7.8, caused the fall of many high buildings and houses in the south of the country. At least 32 persons met their death, and many more are without their homes tonight. At first, the government put out a danger sign for a great sea wave, but they took it away after some hours. The people living there are full of fear, but helpers are working through the night to give food and water.**
*我们先从菲律宾强烈的地震（地球震动）开始。一场震级为7.8级的强烈地震（地球大运动）导致了该国南部许多高楼和房屋的倒塌。至少有32人遇难，今晚还有更多人无家可归。起初，政府发出了巨大海浪（海啸）的危险警告信号，但在几个小时后取消了。住在那里的人们充满了恐惧，但是救援人员（帮助者）正在彻夜工作以提供食物和水。*

**In other news, China's Xi Jinping makes a journey to North Korea to see Kim Jong Un. This is an important political move because such a journey is not common. They had a long talk about working together and giving support to one another. The United States and other countries are looking at this meeting with great care, as it may change the balance of power in that part of the world.**
*在其他新闻方面，中国国家主席习近平前往朝鲜会见金正恩。这是一个重要的政治举动，因为这样的访问并不常见。他们就如何共同合作和相互支持进行了长谈。美国和其他国家正在非常谨慎地关注这次会面，因为它可能会改变世界那个地区的力量平衡。*

**Finally, in Armenia, the government leader Nikol Pashinyan says he had a great win in the political selection of a new government. This selection was seen by many as a test of Russia's power over the country. Pashinyan wants to move his country closer to the West. Even with strong pressure from Russia, the people of Armenia gave their support to his new direction.**
*最后，在亚美尼亚，政府领导人（总理）尼科尔·帕希尼扬表示，他在新政府的政治选举（政治挑选）中取得了巨大胜利。这次选举被许多人视为对俄罗斯对该国影响力的考验。帕希尼扬希望他的国家向西方靠拢。即使在俄罗斯的强大压力下，亚美尼亚人民仍然对他的新方向给予了支持。*
