Morning News Podcast for English Learners

1. Catchy Title
The Daily News Update for English Learners

2. Key Vocabulary
1. Artificial (ar-ti-fi-cial) 人工的
2. Intelligence (in-tel-li-gence) 智能
3. Legislation (le-gis-la-tion) 立法
4. Architecture (ar-chi-tec-ture) 建筑学
5. Inflation (in-fla-tion) 通货膨胀
6. Investment (in-vest-ment) 投资
7. Riot (ri-ot) 暴乱
8. Asset (as-set) 资产

3. Grammar Focus
1. Present Perfect Tense: Used to talk about events that have happened recently with an effect on the present.
2. Passive Voice: Used to focus on the action rather than the person doing the action, common in news reporting.

4. Full Bilingual Podcast Script
