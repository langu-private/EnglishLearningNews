Grammar Focus

1. Present Perfect Tense (现在完成时)
This is used to talk about an action that happened in the past but has a strong connection to the present.
Example: Apple has made a big change in iOS 27.
(苹果在 iOS 27 中做出了重大改变。)

2. Conditional Sentences with If (if 条件句)
This is used to talk about possible future events and their results.
Example: If you have a very old Mac with an Intel engine, it will not get the new changes.
(如果你有一台带有英特尔引擎的非常旧的 Mac，它将无法获得新的改变。)

Full Bilingual Podcast Script

(早上好。很高兴您能和我们一起收听早间新闻谈话。我是 Aria。)

(我是 Andrew。今天很高兴能在这里和大家在一起。我们有很多重要的故事要聊。)

(是的，确实。我们将谈论新机器、重大商业协议以及地球上发生的事件。让我们从关于苹果公司及其语音系统 Siri 的故事开始吧。)

[Andrew] This is a very interesting story, Aria. Many persons have been waiting for a new Siri. They wanted Siri to be more wise and helpful. Now, Apple has made a big change in iOS 27. But why did it take so long?
(这是一个非常有趣的故事，Aria。很多人一直在等待一个新的 Siri。他们希望 Siri 能够更聪明、更有帮助。现在，苹果在 iOS 27 中做出了重大改变。但为什么花了这么长时间呢？)

[Aria] Apple says it is because making a new brain for Siri is very hard. The old Siri only did simple things. If you said, What is the weather this day? it could answer. But if you wanted it to do complex things, it could not. To make Siri wise, they had to build a new system. This system uses a new form of learning for machines.
(苹果表示，这是因为给 Siri 制造一个新大脑非常困难。旧的 Siri 只能做简单的事情。如果你说：“今天天气怎么样？”它能回答。但如果你想让它做复杂的事情，它就无能为力了。为了让 Siri 变得聪明，他们必须建立一个全新的系统。这个系统使用了一种新的机器物理学习形式。)

[Andrew] Yes, and they had to make sure this new brain could run on a small machine, like a pocket machine. They did not want all the thoughts to go to a far-off building with big machines. They wanted the pocket machine to do the work. This keeps the private details of the owner safe. This is a very important point for Apple.
(是的，他们必须确保这个新大脑可以在小型机器上运行，比如口袋机器。他们不希望所有的想法都传到装有大型机器的遥远大楼里。他们希望口袋机器自己来完成这项工作。这样可以保护所有者的私密信息安全。这对苹果来说是非常重要的一点。)

They had to make sure the battery of the pocket machine does not go flat quickly. If a wise system uses too much power, the machine will get warm and stop working.
(确实如此。苹果总是说，保护我们的私密信息安全是他们首要关心的事。但在小型机器上运行智能系统需要很长时间。他们必须测试很多次。他们必须确保口袋机器的电池不会很快耗尽。如果智能系统消耗太多能量，机器就会变热并停止工作。)

[Andrew] I agree. And we also have news about the history of Apple machines. It has been twenty years since Apple made a big decision to use Intel engines in their Mac machines. Before that, they used different engines. But those old engines were too warm and did not have enough power. So, Apple went to Intel.
(我同意。我们还有关于苹果机器历史的新闻。自苹果做出在 Mac 机器中使用英特尔引擎的重大决定以来，已经过去了二十年。在那之前，他们使用不同的引擎。但那些旧引擎太热了，而且动力不足。所以，苹果转向了英特尔。)

(二十年前那是一个非常重大的改变。它帮助苹果制造了更好的 Mac 机器。但是，几年前，苹果又做出了另一个重大改变。他们停止使用英特尔引擎。他们开始制造自己的引擎，称之为 Apple Silicon。他们为什么要这样做？)

[Andrew] Because they wanted more control. They wanted their machines to use less power and stay cold. With Intel, they had to wait for another company to make new engines. With their own engines, they can make changes whenever they want. Now we see that the new macOS system will slowly stop supporting programs made for Intel engines.
(因为他们想要更多的控制权。他们希望自己的机器使用更少的能量并保持低温。使用英特尔，他们必须等待另一家公司制造新引擎。使用自己的引擎，他们可以随时做出改变。现在我们看到新的 macOS 系统将慢慢停止支持为英特尔引擎制作的程序。)

[Aria] This is the end of an old time. If you have a very old Mac with an Intel engine, it will not get the new changes. This shows how fast science and machines move forward. It is necessary to change our machines after some years to keep up with new ideas.
(这是一个旧时代的结束。如果你有一台带有英特尔引擎的非常旧的 Mac，它将无法获得新的改变。这展示了科学和机器向前发展的速度有多快。几年后有必要更换我们的机器，以跟上新思想的步伐。)

[Andrew] Yes, it is. Now, speaking of machines having trouble, let us talk about Google. Google has a wise system called Gemini. But recently, Gemini had a big problem. Many persons who use Android machines found that Gemini could not make calls for them.
(是的，确实如此。现在，说到机器出现问题，让我们来谈谈谷歌。谷歌有一个名为 Gemini 的智能系统。但最近，Gemini 遇到了一个大问题。许多使用安卓机器的人发现 Gemini 无法为他们拨打电话。)

[Aria] Yes, this was a sudden problem. Persons would say, Call my friend, and the machine would say, Something went wrong. This happened on pocket machines and also in carriages that use Android Auto. It was a big trouble for persons who were driving.

[Andrew] Yes, we take them for granted until they break. Now, let us move to the business of play and amusement. Xbox, which is owned by Microsoft, is shutting down a company called Ninja Theory. This company made a very famous play called Hellblade. This news has made many persons sad and angry.
