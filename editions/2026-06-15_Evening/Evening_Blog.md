Grammar Focus

Structure 1: Present Perfect Tense (e.g., "We have seen many changes...")
This structure is used to talk about experiences or events that happened in the past but have a strong connection to the present. In the script, the hosts frequently use this tense to discuss recent news events and their ongoing impact.

Structure 2: Conditional Sentences (e.g., "If the price of oil goes down, the market will...")
This structure is used to talk about possible future situations and their results. It helps ESL learners understand cause-and-effect relationships in news reporting, especially in business and political analysis.
