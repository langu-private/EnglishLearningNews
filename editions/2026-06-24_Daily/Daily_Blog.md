Daily News Podcast: Simple Words for Big Earth News

Key Vocabulary

Agreement (əˈɡriːmənt) - 协定，同意 (An understanding between two or more persons or nations)
Condition (kənˈdɪʃn) - 条件，状态 (The state of something or someone)
Development (dɪˈveləpmənt) - 发展，开发 (The act of growing or making something better)
Discovery (dɪˈskʌvəri) - 发现 (The act of finding something for the first time)
Discussion (dɪˈskʌʃn) - 讨论 (A talk about something with other persons)
Government (ˈɡʌvənmənt) - 政府 (The group of persons who rule a nation)
Knowledge (ˈnɒlɪdʒ) - 知识，了解 (What a person has in his mind about facts)
Observation (ˌɒbzəˈveɪʃn) - 观察 (The act of watching something with care)
Organization (ˌɔːɡənaɪˈzeɪʃn) - 组织，机构 (A group of persons working together for a purpose)
Representative (ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv) - 代表 (A person who acts for other persons)

Grammar Focus

1. Replacing complex verbs with basic operators and nouns. In this talk show, you will hear structures like make a decision instead of decide, have a thought instead of think, and make a journey instead of travel. This is a great way for learning how to say complex ideas with simple words.
2. Conditional sentences with if to talk about future events and possibilities. For example, If the price is low, more persons will have a desire to get the machine. This structure helps us talk about cause and effect in the market.
