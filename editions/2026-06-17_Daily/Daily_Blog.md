Grammar Focus

1. Passive Voice (被动语态)
Example: The company was sold for 2,700,000,000 dollars.
(这家公司以27亿美元的价格被出售。)

2. Conditional Sentences (条件句)
Example: If they have the money, they will get the new glasses.
(如果他们有钱，他们就会买这副新眼镜。)
