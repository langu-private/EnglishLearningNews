### 🎯 Grammar Focus (语法焦点)

1.  **Simple Present Tense for News Reporting (一般现在时用于新闻报道)**
    *   News often uses the simple present tense to state facts, current events, or ongoing situations, making the information feel immediate and relevant.
    *   **Example from script:** "[Aria] Apple, a very big company, **had** a special meeting. It **was** called WWDC 2026. ... The big news **is** about iOS 27. This **is** the new brain for your iPhone."
    *   **Explanation:** While the meeting "had" and "was called" in the past, the "big news is" and "this is" iOS 27 are presented as current facts or ongoing relevance. This mix is common in news to connect past events to their present implications.

2.  **Using "To Be" for Definitions and Descriptions (使用“是”进行定义和描述)**
    *   In simple English, the verb "to be" (is, are, was, were) is frequently used to define terms, describe states, or identify subjects, which is crucial for ESL learners to grasp new concepts.
    *   **Example from script:** "[Andrew] This meeting **is** a big day for Apple. It **is** a time when they show new ideas. ... Siri **is** the voice helper in your Apple phone."
    *   **Explanation:** This structure helps to clearly introduce and explain what something is, making complex topics more accessible.
