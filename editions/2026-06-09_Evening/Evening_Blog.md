📖 **Key Vocabulary**

1.  **Authority** (ɔːˈθɒrɪti) - 权力，权威
    *   *Meaning:* The power or right to give orders, make decisions, and make people obey.
    *   *Example:* The police have the authority to keep people safe.
2.  **Condition** (kənˈdɪʃən) - 条件，状况
    *   *Meaning:* The state of something, especially with regard to its appearance, quality, or working order.
    *   *Example:* The car is in good condition.
3.  **Process** (ˈprəʊsɛs) - 过程，程序
    *   *Meaning:* A series of actions or steps taken in order to achieve a particular end.
    *   *Example:* Making bread is a long process.
4.  **Effect** (ɪˈfɛkt) - 影响，效果
    *   *Meaning:* A change that is a result or consequence of an action or other cause.
    *   *Example:* The rain had a good effect on the plants.
5.  **System** (ˈsɪstɪm) - 系统，制度
    *   *Meaning:* A set of things working together as parts of a mechanism or an interconnecting network.
    *   *Example:* The body has many systems, like the blood system.
6.  **Control** (kənˈtrəʊl) - 控制
    *   *Meaning:* The power to direct or limit something.
    *   *Example:* The driver has control of the car.
7.  **Demand** (dɪˈmɑːnd) - 需求
    *   *Meaning:* A strong request; or the desire of buyers for a good or service.
    *   *Example:* There is a high demand for new phones.
8.  **Conflict** (ˈkɒnflɪkt) - 冲突，争执
    *   *Meaning:* A serious disagreement or argument, often a long one.
    *   *Example:* The two countries had a long conflict.

🎯 **Grammar Focus**

1.  **Present Perfect Tense (e.g., "has changed," "have seen")**
    *   Used to talk about actions that started in the past and continue to the present, or actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past but have a present effect.
    *   *Example:* "Apple **has made** many new things for a long time." (苹果长期以来制造了许多新产品。)
    *   *Example:* "We **have seen** big changes in the world." (我们看到了世界的巨大变化。)

2.  **Conditional Sentences (Type 1: If + present simple, will + base verb)**
    *   Used to talk about real and possible situations in the future.
    *   *Example:* "If you **use** the new system, you **will find** it easy." (如果你使用新系统，你会觉得它很容易。)
    *   *Example:* "If the market **is** good, the company **will get** more money." (如果市场好，公司会得到更多的钱。)
