Daily News Podcast: Simple English Learning with Global Stories

Key Vocabulary

destruction /dɪˈstrʌkʃn/ (noun) - 破坏 (The act of causing great damage or ruin)
agreement /əˈɡriːmənt/ (noun) - 协议 (A decision made by two or more groups to work together)
behavior /bɪˈheɪvjə/ (noun) - 行为 (The way a person or thing acts or conducts itself)
conscious /ˈkɒnʃəs/ (adjective) - 有意识的 (Being awake and aware of what is happening around you)
authority /ɔːˈθɒrəti/ (noun) - 权威/当局 (The power or right to give orders and make decisions)
representative /ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/ (noun) - 代表 (A person chosen to speak or act for others)
adjustment /əˈdʒʌstmənt/ (noun) - 调整 (A small change made to make something work better)

Grammar Focus

1. Present Perfect Tense for Recent Events
We use the present perfect tense (have/has + past participle) to talk about recent events that have a strong connection to the present moment.
Example in script: China has invested a very large amount of money in domestic AI.
(中国在国内AI领域投入了巨额资金。)

2. Conditional Sentences with If for Cause and Effect
We use conditional sentences to explain how one event causes another event to happen.
Example in script: If the price of gas falls, families will have more money for food.
(如果汽油价格下降，家庭将有更多的钱买食物。)
