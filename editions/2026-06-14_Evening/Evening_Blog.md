Grammar Focus

1. Expressing Ability with Be Able To
In Basic English, the word can is not used. Instead, we use be able to to show ability.
Example: You are able to fly like a bird with this new machine. (你能够用这个新机器像鸟一样飞翔。)

2. Present Perfect Tense for Recent Events
We use have or has with the past form of a action word to talk about things that happened recently.
Example: Qatari mediators have traveled to Tehran. (卡塔尔调解人已经前往德黑兰。)
