Morning News for English Learners

Key Vocabulary

Agreement /uh-gree-muhnt/ (协定，协议) - A decision made between two or more groups or nations.
Decision /dih-sizh-uhn/ (决定) - A choice that you make after thinking.
Government /guhv-ern-muhnt/ (政府) - The group of people who rule a country.
Condition /kuhn-dish-uhn/ (条件，状况) - The state that something is in.
Danger /dayn-jer/ (危险) - Something that may cause hurt or damage.
Process /proh-ses/ (过程，步骤) - A series of actions to do something.
Limit /lim-it/ (限制，限度) - A line or point that you must not go past.
Disease /dih-zeez/ (疾病) - An illness of the body.

Grammar Focus

1. Present Perfect Tense (现在完成时)
We use the present perfect tense to talk about an action that happened in the past but has a strong connection to the present. It is formed with have or has and the past form of the verb.
Example: The government has made a new rule. (政府已经制定了一条新规则。)
Example: Many people have lost their money. (许多人已经失去了他们的钱。)

2. Conditional Sentences with If (if 引导的条件句)
We use if to talk about a possible action and its result. In simple English, we keep the structure clear to show cause and effect.
Example: If you use this machine, you will see the change. (如果你使用这台机器，你会看到变化。)
Example: If the price of oil goes down, the market will be happy. (如果石油价格下跌，市场将会很高兴。)
