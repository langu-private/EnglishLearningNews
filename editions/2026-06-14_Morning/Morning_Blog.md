Morning News Podcast for English Learners

Key Vocabulary

1. Apparatus /æpəˈreɪtəs/ (noun) - 仪器，设备 (A complex machine or instrument used for a specific purpose)
2. Authority /ɔːˈθɒrəti/ (noun) - 权力，官方机构 (The power or right to give orders, make decisions, and enforce obedience)
3. Condition /kənˈdɪʃn/ (noun) - 状况，条件 (The state of something with regard to its appearance, quality, or working order)
4. Destruction /dɪˈstrʌkʃn/ (noun) - 破坏，毁灭 (The action of causing so much damage to something that it no longer exists)
5. Discovery /dɪˈskʌvəri/ (noun) - 发现 (The act of finding or learning something for the first time)
6. Expansion /ɪkˈspænʃn/ (noun) - 扩张，膨胀 (The action of becoming larger or more extensive)
7. Instrument /ˈɪnstrəmənt/ (noun) - 工具，仪器 (A tool or implement, especially one for precision work)
8. Organization /ˌɔːɡənaɪˈzeɪʃn/ (noun) - 组织，机构 (An organized group of people with a particular purpose)
9. Representative /ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/ (noun) - 代表 (A person chosen or appointed to act or speak for others)
10. Structure /ˈstrʌktʃə(r)/ (noun) - 结构，建筑物 (The arrangement of and relations between the parts or elements of something complex)

Grammar Focus

1. The Use of "Make" + Object + Adjective / Verb (Causative Structure)
In Basic English, we frequently use "make" to express causation because many specific causative verbs are not on the 850-word list. For example: "This makes the machine seem very simple" (这让机器显得非常简单) or "They want to make their voice clear" (他们想让自己的声音清晰). This is a highly useful structure for ESL learners to express complex actions using simple vocabulary.

2. Expressing Complex Nouns through Prepositional Phrases and Relative Clauses
Since the 850-word list lacks many specialized nouns (like "drone", "trillionaire", or "merger"), the script uses descriptive phrases. For example, instead of "drone", we say "flying machine with no man in it" (没有人在里面的飞行机器). Instead of "trillionaire", we say "man with a million million dollars" (拥有万亿美金的人). This teaches learners how to circumlocute—an essential skill in language acquisition when vocabulary is limited.
