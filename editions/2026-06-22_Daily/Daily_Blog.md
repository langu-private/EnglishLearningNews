Daily News Podcast: Future Machines, Money Decisions, and Earth Events in Simple English

Key Vocabulary

Apparatus (noun) - /ˌæpəˈreɪtəs/ - 设备，仪器 (A complex machine or instrument used for a specific purpose)
Agreement (noun) - /əˈɡriːmənt/ - 协定，协议 (A decision or arrangement made between two or more groups)
Condition (noun) - /kənˈdɪʃən/ - 条件，状态 (The state of something, or the rules under which something happens)
Decision (noun) - /dɪˈsɪʒən/ - 决定，决议 (A choice made after thinking about different options)
Discovery (noun) - /dɪˈskʌvəri/ - 发现 (The act of finding something new)
Distribution (noun) - /ˌdɪstrɪˈbjuːʃən/ - 分配，分发 (The act of giving things out to different places or persons)
Expansion (noun) - /ɪkˈspænʃən/ - 扩张，膨胀 (The act of getting larger in size or amount)
Representative (noun) - /ˌreprɪˈzentətɪv/ - 代表 (A person who speaks or acts for a group of other persons)

Grammar Focus

1. The use of "make + noun + adjective/verb" to show cause and effect.
Example in news: "iOS 27 will make older iPhones run faster." (iOS 27将使旧款iPhone运行得更快。)
This structure is very useful for ESL learners to explain how new designs or decisions change the condition of things.

2. The use of "be able to" for future capability.
Example in news: "We will be able to see what the machine sees." (我们将能够看到机器所看到的东西。)
This is a simple way to talk about future possibilities without using complex modal verbs.
