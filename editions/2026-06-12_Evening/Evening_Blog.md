3. 🎯 Grammar Focus.
1. Present Perfect Tense: Used to discuss recent news events that have current relevance (e.g., "Apple has made a new choice").
2. Conditional Sentences: Used to analyze potential future outcomes of business mergers or political changes (e.g., "If they join, the market will change").
