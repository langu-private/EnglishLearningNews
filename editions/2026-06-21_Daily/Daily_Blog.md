Grammar Focus

Structure 1: The use of make + noun/adjective to express actions. In Basic English, we do not have many verbs. We use make to form actions. For example, we say make a decision instead of decide, or make clean instead of clean. This is a very useful way to speak simply.

Structure 2: Conditional sentences with if and will. We use this structure to talk about a possible future event and its result. For example, If the price is high, we will have trouble. This is very common in business and daily talk.
