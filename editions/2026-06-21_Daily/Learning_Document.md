## 2. Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)

Here are 8 key words and phrases from the podcast to elevate your English vocabulary:

### 1. Standalone /ˌstænd.əˈloʊn/
*   **Meaning**: adj. 独立的（不依赖其他系统或设备的）
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"One feature is a new standalone app. It is a key feature that now has its own place."*
*   **Example Sentence**: The company released a standalone version of its popular photo editing tool.

### 2. Volatile /ˈvɑː.lə.t̬əl/
*   **Meaning**: adj. 易变的，动荡不定的，不稳定的
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"Space is hard, and the market can be very volatile."*
*   **Example Sentence**: Cryptocurrency prices are highly volatile, making them a risky investment.

### 3. Under water /ˌʌn.dɚ ˈwɑː.t̬ɚ/
*   **Meaning**: adj. (金融) 亏损的，资不抵债的（股票或房产现值低于买入价/贷款额）
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"So, the average buyer who got the stock after the IPO is almost under water now."*
*   **Example Sentence**: After the housing market crashed, many homeowners found themselves under water on their mortgages.

### 4. Stagflation /stæɡˈfleɪ.ʃən/
*   **Meaning**: n. 滞胀（经济停滞、高失业率与通货膨胀并存的经济现象）
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"Who wins in China? The AI supercycle or domestic stagflation?"*
*   **Example Sentence**: Economists fear that rising energy prices could plunge the country into a period of stagflation.

### 5. Penchant /ˈpen.tʃənt/
*   **Meaning**: n. 偏好，嗜好，倾向 (usually followed by "for")
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"Warsh makes his case with jargon and a penchant for detail."*
*   **Example Sentence**: She has a penchant for buying vintage clothing and antique furniture.

### 6. Quadruple /kwɑːˈdruː.pəl/
*   **Meaning**: v. （使）成四倍
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"Some reports say the memory market in 2026 will quadruple compared to 2025."*
*   **Example Sentence**: The company's profits quadrupled in just two years thanks to the new product line.

### 7. Evacuate /ɪˈvæk.ju.eɪt/
*   **Meaning**: v. 撤离，疏散
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"In addition, almost one thousand and seven hundred tourists had to be evacuated from the resort."*
*   **Example Sentence**: Police had to evacuate the building after a suspicious package was found.

### 8. Critical condition /ˈkrɪt̬.ɪ.kəl kənˈdɪʃ.ən/
*   **Meaning**: n. 极度危险的状态，（伤势/病情）危重
*   **Example in Podcast**: *"In addition, nine people remain in critical condition in the hospital."*
*   **Example Sentence**: The driver was rushed to the hospital and is currently in critical condition.

## 3. Useful Expressions (实用表达)

### 1. Put all one's eggs in one basket (不要把鸡蛋放在同一个篮子里)
*   **Explanation**: To risk everything on a single venture or decision. (分散风险，不要孤注一掷)
*   **Podcast Context**: *"So, investors must be very careful. They should not put all their money in one basket."*
*   **Example**: When investing in the stock market, diversify your portfolio; never put all your eggs in one basket.

### 2. Put someone on the spot (使某人处于难堪/尴尬的境地)
*   **Explanation**: To force someone into a position where they must make a difficult decision or answer a difficult question immediately. (让人当场表态或陷入难堪)
*   **Podcast Context**: *"On a phone call, you have to answer immediately... They feel put on the spot."*
*   **Example**: The reporter put the politician on the spot by asking about the leaked emails.

### 3. Walled garden effect (围墙花园效应)
*   **Explanation**: A closed ecosystem where the service provider controls all applications, content, and media, keeping users restricted within their platform. (生态闭环，让用户产生依赖而不愿离开)
*   **Podcast Context**: *"Once you are inside, you do not want to leave... It is called the walled garden effect."*
*   **Example**: Apple's App Store is a classic example of a walled garden, offering high security but limited freedom.

### 4. Friendly fire (友军炮火/误伤)
*   **Explanation**: Weapon fire coming from one's own side, especially that which causes accidental injury or death to one's own forces. (军事上的误伤，或比喻来自同盟者的伤害)
*   **Podcast Context**: *"Some reports suggest the blast may have been caused by friendly fire, but we do not know for sure."*
*   **Example**: The soldier was tragically killed by friendly fire during the chaotic night battle.
