3. 🎯 Grammar Focus:
- Present Perfect Tense: Used to describe actions that started in the past and continue to the present (e.g., "The market has seen many changes").
- Conditional Sentences: Used to talk about possible future events (e.g., "If the price goes up, the company will lose money").
