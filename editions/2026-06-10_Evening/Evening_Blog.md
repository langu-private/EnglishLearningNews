1. A Catchy Title
The Daily News Talk: Apple Tech, World War Fears, and Money News

2. Key Vocabulary
1. Inflation (in-fla-shun) - 通货膨胀
2. Volatility (vol-a-til-i-ty) - 波动性
3. Reserve (re-serve) - 储备
4. Security (se-cu-ri-ty) - 安全
5. Negotiation (ne-go-ti-a-tion) - 谈判
6. Domestic (do-mes-tic) - 国内的
7. License (li-cense) - 执照
8. Artificial (ar-ti-fi-cial) - 人工的

3. Grammar Focus
1. Present Perfect Tense: Used to talk about actions that started in the past and continue to the present (e.g., "The price has gone up").
2. Passive Voice: Used to focus on the action rather than the person doing it (e.g., "The cards are being removed").
