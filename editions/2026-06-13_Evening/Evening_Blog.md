Podcast Show Notes

1. Catchy Title: Global News Update and English Learning Hour

2. Key Vocabulary
- Acquire /əˈkwaɪər/ 获得
- Logistics /ləˈdʒɪstɪks/ 物流
- Prohibit /prəˈhɪbɪt/ 禁止
- Merger /ˈmɜːrdʒər/ 合并
- Stability /stəˈbɪləti/ 稳定性
- Technical /ˈteknɪkl/ 技术的
- Violent /ˈvaɪələnt/ 暴力的
- Significant /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/ 重要的

3. Grammar Focus
- Passive Voice: Used to describe actions where the subject is not the focus (e.g., The models were disabled by the company).
- Conditional Sentences: Used to discuss possible outcomes of events (e.g., If the government makes a rule, the company must follow it).
